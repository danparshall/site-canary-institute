P101 Run lineage, splitting and laundering

Can split, resumed or laundered training runs be linked to one model, at a stated false-link rate?

Linking split, resumed or laundered runs to one model.

Status

Started Experiments on GPT-2 (124M parameters, OpenWebText) and published Pythia checkpoints: a glued transcript (two training runs reported as one continuous run, i.e. a run resumed from another run's checkpoint) is caught by a weight-norm jump and a memorization cut-off at the join; the verifier holds data, weights, code and checkpoints. The opposite direction (one long run reported as many 'fresh' short runs) rests on the certified-random initialisation design, argued hard to spoof but not tested; no false-link rate at frontier scale; laundering of a checkpoint through declared 'training data' not covered.

Below, we show that our methods address existing attacks from the literature (Glue-ing and Interpolation), and also demonstrate our method’s response to two new attacks (Data Addition and Subtraction).
Choi, Shavit & Duvenaud (2023), Tools for Verifying Neural Models' Training Data, arXiv 2307.00682 (2023-07-02), L724
