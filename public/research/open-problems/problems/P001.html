P001 Seals and tamper-evident monitoring of servers and racks

What detection and false-accept rates do tamper-evident seals, tags and camera/sensor suites achieve on chips, servers and racks under skilled covert attack?

Detection rates of tamper-evident seals, tags and camera/sensor suites on chips, servers and racks (not tamper-respondent enclosures).

Status

Started Amodo's own status report of a built tray-level retrofit sensor suite (cameras with OpenCV intrusion detection, PIR, temperature, pressure, IMU, tamper mesh, lid switch, time-of-flight; STM32 TrustZone controller; power-down response) in an RFD still marked 'idea'; no detection or false-alarm rate, no skilled-attacker test (red-team and stress testing are listed as future work), no dataset. The seals clause has no held test: the 289-seal assessment that found all could be defeated cheaply is named by Aarne, Fist & Withers (2024) but not held, so it is not Failed here; 'TamperSec working on this (?)' in the Proofworks projects list is hearsay; Harack et al.'s glitter-and-photograph suggestion is untested. The headline's 'under skilled covert attack' clause has no work.

Amodo's v0.1 system includes 8 countermeasures selected from 38 candidates after reviewing 27 attack vectors. … Cameras (4 per tray, OpenCV-based intrusion detection) … Successfully demonstrated detection using STM built-in logic
