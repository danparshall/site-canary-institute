P111 Escalation and attribution of findings

What escalation ladder, graded-evidence aggregation and attribution procedure resolve ambiguous findings and assign a violation to the responsible party?

Escalation ladders for ambiguous findings, aggregation of graded evidence, and attribution of a violation to the responsible party.

Status

Analyzed A draft treaty article (Article XI, modelled on Articles IX, XII and XIV of the Chemical Weapons Convention): notification, 36-hour acknowledgement and 5-day clarification, Executive Council adjudication with a challenge inspection, then graded measures (added monitoring, hardware relinquishment, sanctions, protective actions); a proposal, untested. Aggregating graded evidence (many sub-threshold acts) and attributing a violation to the responsible actor (cloud provider versus customer, runs split across providers) have no design; Moon et al.'s call for an agency able to unmask evaders is framing.

The Concerned Party notifies the Requested Party of their concern, while also sharing their concern with the Director-General and Executive Council. The Requested Party will acknowledge this notification within 36 hours, and provide clarification within 5 days.
Scher, Abecassis, Barnett & Abeyta (2025), An International Agreement to Prevent the Premature Creation of Artificial Superintelligence (MIRI), arXiv 2511.10783 v3 (2026-05-08), L2140–2143
