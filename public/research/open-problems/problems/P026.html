P026 Location verification of chips

What location precision and false-accept rate can network-delay geolocation of accelerators achieve against an adversary who adds delay, relays, or spoofs?

Delay-based and network-measurement geolocation and co-location of accelerators against adversarial delay, relays and spoofing.

Terms: N: the number of chips taking part in a co-location challenge.

Status

Started The authors' own measurement of their own method on rented cloud GPUs with cooperative hosts: network-delay geolocation against public RIPE Atlas landmarks places a B200 within 44 km and a V100 within 270 km (79-298 km depending on landmark density) and rejects 11 of 11 decoy sites; the decoys are other locations, not an adversary adding delay or relaying, and the paper says a high round-trip time is no evidence of absence from a forbidden region and the scheme is not tamper-proof, so the headline's false-accept rate against added delay, relays or spoofing is not reported. Timed co-location challenges across N chips: no work. Robustness of on-chip GPS to commercial spoofers is a related sub-question, not in the headline, with no work.

169 RIPE Atlas probes place the B200 within 44 km of its claimed datacentre and reject all 11 decoy sites.
Alpay & Alpay (2026), Unprivileged Topology Certificates for Cloud GPU Attestation, arXiv 2606.24934 (2026-06-22), L35–36
