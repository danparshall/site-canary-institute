P105 Model identity across evaluation and deployment

Can a verifier confirm the evaluated, deployed and served models are the same (e.g. via attested weight/code hash) in a real pilot?

Showing evaluated, deployed and served models are the same.

Status

Started Served-model identity by recomputation on one 8xH100 server with prover and verifier on the same box; open-weight 7B-120B models; developer-run attacks (model substitution, LoRA append, append-on-trigger); 37,500 prompt runs; a fixed threshold gave honest-prover failures and short-output false negatives. The evaluated-versus-deployed clause (a TEE-signed hash of weights and code) is a design only, which Scher & Thiergart call 'mostly solved in principle'; no real pilot, which the headline asks for; handling of legitimate model versions: no work.

Our results add further evidence that Token DiFR can identify different incorrect-model attacks reliably across a small range of prompts.
Amodo (2026), An Inference Verification Prototype — Stage 1 (Amodo Design note) (2026-06-29), L235
