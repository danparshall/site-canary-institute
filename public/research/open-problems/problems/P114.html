P114 End-to-end pilots, demonstrations and red-team exercises

What have published pilots and red-team exercises of the verification stack (including two-state experiments) detected, leaked, and how fast did they warn?

Running and publishing prototypes, pilots and red-team events of the verification stack, including two-state experiments.

Status

Started One 8xH100 server with prover and verifier on the same box; open-weight 7B-120B models; inference only; developer-run attacks (model substitution, LoRA append, append-on-trigger); 37,500 prompt runs; completeness ignored; a fixed threshold gave honest-prover failures and short-output false negatives. Not an independent red team, no national-security team, no two-state experiment, no leak or warning-time measurement; Reynolds & Foster's line-rate hashing benchmark is a second self-run component test. Red-team exercises of the stack and a joint international experiment: no work (Baker et al., early 2025: the listed challenges 'mostly receive little to no work').

Our results add further evidence that Token DiFR can identify different incorrect-model attacks reliably across a small range of prompts.
Amodo (2026), An Inference Verification Prototype — Stage 1 (Amodo Design note) (2026-06-29), L235
