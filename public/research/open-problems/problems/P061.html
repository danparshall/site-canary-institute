P061 Verifiable memory wipes and memory challenges

Can a node's HBM/DRAM/SSD and network-equipment memory be proven wiped or filled within a stated time and challenge latency, and how much non-wipeable storage remains?

Proving HBM/DRAM/SSD and network-equipment memory is wiped or filled: wipe time, non-wipeable storage, challenge latency and soundness.

Status

Started Proof-of-secure-erasure experiments on H200 hardware, the authors' own report: RAM and HBM wipe estimated at tens of minutes for a GB200 tray; disk wipe about 24 hours for 15 TB, extrapolated from a measured 1 TB NVMe write; challenge-phase time not yet computed; non-wipeable memory targeted below 1 GiB but not yet quantified; network-switch memory listed as open. Challenge clause: Monfared et al. 2026 measured a gap above 350 ms between HBM-resident and host-RAM-resident 60 GB challenges on an H100, with no false-alarm rates; Reuter 2026's roughly 2x margin against host-DRAM caching is an analysis; Bursuc et al. 2024 give provably secure proofs of erasure with round-trip-time bounds (theory). Cankaya's v0.2 draft (dated) says a network-level timing probe telling one node's DRAM from another's 'has not yet been demonstrated'. Both clauses sit in one memory-verification community; not every clause answered.

Writing a full 1 TB gave a final average write speed of 160 MiB/s — much lower than the advertised rate — taking ~1h 35 minutes.
Reynolds & Jacobi (2026), Memory Wipes - Performance Analysis (Amodo Design Notes) (2026-07-01), L269
