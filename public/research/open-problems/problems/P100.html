P100 Compute-threshold definitions and loopholes

What compute counts toward thresholds, how thresholds are set, moved and renegotiated (including regional caps and coverage exemptions), and how runs evade them.

Status

Analyzed Evasion clause only: a simulated cost of structuring a 10^24, 10^25 or 10^26 FLOP run as clusters below every proposed registration threshold (DiLoCo-family methods over 100 Mbps links; extrapolated 10-300x beyond tested model size). The same paper proposes a 1,280 GB HBM memory threshold as a fix to the threshold definition, also paper only.
