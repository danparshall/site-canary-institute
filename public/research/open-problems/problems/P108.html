P108 Inspection triggers and the will to invoke them

What should trigger an inspection, and how can inspections still happen when the suspect state has a great-power patron?

What triggers an inspection, and whether inspections happen at all when the suspect state has a great-power patron or the host state denies cooperation.

Status

Analyzed A proposed design (automatic compute-anomaly triggers inside a three-layer inspectorate) that answers the trigger clause on paper; the great-power-patron clause rests on the authors' reading of the historical record, and their analysis of challenge inspections in the Alva Myrdal Centre treaty database is not yet reported; no false-alarm rate and no trigger calibration, which the authors call 'the substantive work ahead'. The Proofworks observation that no challenge inspections have been implemented between great powers and Koopmanschap & Barten's point that power monitoring needs utility cooperation are context and a stated limit.

formal challenge inspections historically go uninvoked when a violator has a great-power patron, so verification cannot depend on a state being willing to accuse.
