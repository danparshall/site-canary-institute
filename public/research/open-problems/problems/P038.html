P038 Facility power models

How large is the error in facility- and distribution-level power models when the operator chooses workloads to mislead them?

Validity of facility- and PDU-level power models under adversarial choice.

Terms: PDU (power distribution unit): the rack-level equipment that feeds and meters power to servers; a facility/PDU power model estimates compute from power readings at those points.

Status

Analyzed Honest operator; facility power modelled, not metered (the NLR dataset combines measured workload profiles with facility power from a bottom-up model of the power-delivery components); 2-16 Kestrel nodes of four H100s each running Llama-2 70B LoRA and Stable Diffusion: a node-count report from mean facility power tolerates about plus or minus 15% uniform calibration error. Adversarial workload choice, the headline's condition, is not modelled at facility or distribution level. Cross-references, not evidence, because the headline names facility scale: Kimpson, Baker & Graham's adversarial power bound is for a single A100 and the paper says it offers no facility-level, multi-device version; Rahman & Tajdari measured wall versus NVML power on one workstation under evasion strategies.

When every power value is multiplied by the same calibration factor, the fixed quantizer remains perfect from −15.5% to +18.5% error; failures begin at −16% and +19%. This sweep measures tolerance to uniform calibration error.
Abdelghafar & Kulp (2026), Privacy-Preserving AI Verification via Minimal Information Disclosure, arXiv 2608.02774 (2026-08-03), L1115–1117
