P083 Inference verification overhead

How much latency does inference verification add, and how much serving throughput does it cost?

The latency, serving throughput, verifier compute and record size that deploying inference verification imposes.

Status

Started The authors' own measurements on Llama-2-13B (Table 6): committing overhead 0.26 ms per token, validation time 81 ms, 10 KB of provider GPU memory per token; a measured per-token latency cost, not serving throughput at production batch sizes or end-to-end latency on a deployed service. Amodo's Stage-1 prototype measures verifier GPU utilisation (about 2% versus about 53% for the prover on one H100 tray, small open-weight models) and lists latency and throughput as future work; Karvonen et al. (DiFR) measure record size (1.5 to 30 bytes per token), which is in the statement, not the headline.
