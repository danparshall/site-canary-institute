P044 Remote sensing of facilities

Can remote sensing (optical, thermal, radar, seismic) estimate a data centre's power use or find undeclared sites, within what error and at what false-positive rate?

Remote sensing (EO, thermal, SAR, seismic) of data centres.

Terms: EO: electro-optical (visible-light imagery). SAR: synthetic-aperture radar (all-weather radar imaging). MWIR: mid-wave infrared (a thermal band). MASINT: measurement-and-signature intelligence (detection from physical signatures).

Status

Analyzed A desk analysis of the visual signature by facility size: cooling-tower arrays (economic above 5–10 MW) on a 2,000 m² footprint are anomalous against EIA building statistics; at 1 MW air-cooled chillers and at 100 kW a container look like ordinary commercial buildings. 'Find undeclared sites' clause only, with no false-positive rate and no search without prior candidates; the 'estimate power use within what error' clause has no held work; thermal, radar and seismic clauses are treated qualitatively (seismic sensing 'a confirmation tool, not a search tool'; vibration dampening could defeat it). The classifier result the authors cite (Clymer 2025: all facilities above 10 MW found, all four below missed) is not held and is not evidence here.

"Satellite surveillance requires no cooperation from the target state, making it deployable unilaterally. It is most useful as a first filter for facilities at 10,000 H100-equivalents and above, with corroboration from other measures."
Koopmanschap & Barten (2026), How to Catch a GPU: A Taxonomy of Verification and Enforcement Mechanisms for International AI Agreements (arXiv 2607.22619v1), App. B, Satellite surveillance (2026 (arXiv 2607.22619v1; stamp reads 16 Jun 2026)), L826–828
