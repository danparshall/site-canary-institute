P122 Field capacity, funding and deployment readiness

What does the verification field need, in funding, people and manufacturing capacity, to reach deployment, and how does that compare with what exists?

Field funding, capacity and deployment readiness.

Status

Analyzed Funding only, as a proponent's unsourced figures: a $50 to 150M capitalisation recommended for one central project, against 'less than $15M' spent on verification technologies to date (same document, AI-lab section). People and manufacturing capacity are not estimated: the companion SITREP lists 'preparing plans for quick and secure scaled-up manufacturing' as an extension and Proofworks notes a multi-month manufacturing buffer. No survey of the field's funding or headcount.

Fund a large central project to “own” Plan A verification. Their goal should be to ensure AI verification technology can be deployed in early 2029. The remit would cover not just the R&D but also the stockpiling and planning for scale-out. Before the end of 2026, they should be capitalised with at least $50-150M, and as the project succeeds, they should expect to scale to absorb more funding.
Amodo Design (2026), AI 2040 Plan A: How You Can Help (2026-07-02), L124
