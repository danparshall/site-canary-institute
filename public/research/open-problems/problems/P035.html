P035 Capture volume, storage and recomputation infrastructure

What capture bandwidth, storage and recomputation infrastructure does tap-based verification require, and can sampling cut it to a feasible level?

Capture volume, storage and recomputation infrastructure.

Status

Analyzed Desk estimates of the quantities asked: sampling 0.01% of a scalable unit's 256 400G scale-out links through a 1x256 optical circuit switch to one FPGA, about $25,000 per unit or about 0.2% of an inferred $12M unit price; storage-fabric capture sized from bursty traffic (about 40 GB/s peak, 3 GB/s average); Amodo's SITREP gives a worst case of one recomputation NIC per inference front-end NIC and tags sampling taps 'Not started'; the author reports that typical north-south edge traffic composition was unavailable to him. Nothing built or measured; this survey's cost goal of 1% of facility cost is met only on paper.

A sampling rate of 0.01% of the compute fabric traffic would mean about 20Gb/s of traffic to timestamp and hash (and maybe parse).
Cankaya (2026), Research Note: The Fundamentals and Feasibility of Secure Network Taps for Verifying AI Datacenter Use (Substack), appendix EW cost estimate for inter-SU compute-fabric taps (2026-04-30 (live page read 2026-10-21)), L340
