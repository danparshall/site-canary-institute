P015 Advanced packaging (chiplets, 3D stacking)

Can covert channels, untrusted-die key access, or die substitution be detected in chiplet / 3D-stacked packages at a demonstrated rate?

Security of chiplets and 3D-stacked packages.

Terms: chiplet: a chip built from several smaller dies packaged together. 3D stacking: stacking dies vertically in one package. UCIe: an industry standard for linking chiplets inside a package.
