P023 Residual covert-channel capacity out of monitored compute

How many bits per second or per token survive monitoring via steganography, protocol free variables, and physical channels (fibre, coolant, RF)?

Measured bits/s or bits/token that survive monitoring: steganography in outputs and egress, protocol free variables, and physical channels (fibre, coolant, RF).

Status

Started Steganography in inference outputs only: the authors' own detector on open-weight models of 3B-30B parameters under their trust assumptions (every input, output and seed logged; honest verification server). Protocol free variables: Cankaya's Fermi estimate of about 100 bits per second through active north-south taps, which he calls informed guesses needing validation - paper only. Physical channels (fibre, coolant, RF): Cankaya's side-channel survey estimates ('we expect Mbps to be possible'; 'strong case studies are few') - paper only; NVLink contention: no work. Older covert-channel measurements in the collection (Ethernet padding, thermal) were not made against compute monitoring.

On MOE-Qwen-30B, our detector reduces exfiltratable information to < 0.5% with false-positive rate of < 10−2 , corresponding to a > 200× slowdown for adversaries.
Rinberg, Karvonen, Hoover, Reuter & Warr (2025), Verifying LLM Inference to Detect Model Weight Exfiltration (arXiv 2511.02620v3) (2026-03-12), L37–39
