P041 Grid sensing infrastructure and hard caps

What grid-sensing infrastructure exists (digital relays, phasor sensors, control-system deadbands) and what hard regulatory caps bound a hidden load's size?

Grid sensing infrastructure and hard caps that bound hidden load.

Terms: PMU (phasor measurement unit): a sensor that samples grid voltage and current very precisely and time-stamped. SCADA: a utility's supervisory control-and-data system; a 'deadband' is a change too small for it to report. LEA (low-energy analog) sensor: a voltage or current sensor whose small analog output feeds a protective relay directly (IEEE C37.92). Rogowski coil: a clip-on current sensor.
