P090 Proof-of-work and proof of declared non-AI use

Can a verifier confirm privately, at a small fraction of the job's cost, that a cluster ran a declared non-AI or useful workload?

Proof that compute ran a declared non-AI or useful workload.

Terms: MatMul(A, B): multiplying matrix A by matrix B. 1 + o(1) overhead: a cost that approaches that of plain matrix multiplication as the matrices grow (o(1) is a term that shrinks toward zero).

Status

Analyzed A proof-of-useful-work protocol for matrix multiplication over a finite field with near-zero prover overhead; security rests on a new hardness conjecture; no implementation, benchmark or attack evaluation. It proves a lower bound on matmul work done, not that a declared workload ran, and says nothing about privacy or about non-AI scientific workloads (weather, CFD, protein folding), which have no work. The Proofworks proof-of-memory protocol (Reuter & Karvonen 2025, preliminary fingerprint experiments) verifies declared AI workloads generally and is a neighbouring compute-accounting problem.

"Our main result is a PoUW for the task of Matrix Multiplication MatMul(A, B) of arbitrary matrices with 1 + o(1) multiplicative overhead compared to naïve MatMul. We conjecture that our protocol has optimal security in the sense that a malicious prover cannot obtain any significant advantage over an honest prover."
Komargodski & Weinstein (2025), Proofs of Useful Work from Arbitrary Matrix Multiplication (arXiv 2504.09971v4), Abstract (2025-11-13), L17–20
