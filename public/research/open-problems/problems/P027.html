P027 Enforcing and attesting cluster composition and interconnect limits

Can hardware fix which devices may link, cap inter-device bandwidth, and attest cluster size against bridging devices, at what training slowdown?

Hardware that fixes which devices may link, caps inter-device bandwidth, and attests cluster size/configuration against bridging devices.

Status

Started Amodo's own report of its own work, not independently checked: a central controller leases bandwidth and transfer-volume quotas to BlueField DPUs over an encrypted tunnel, the DPUs enforce them in software with the Linux traffic controller, and the system runs on one research server; hardware acceleration on BlueField-2/3 is listed as in progress. Not shown: a hardware-fixed set of permitted peers, attestation of cluster size against a bridging device or reconfiguration, holding a cap at line rate (the RFD lists 'can software tc keep up?' as open), and any training slowdown. Fixing which devices may link and attesting cluster size: paper designs only (RAND 2024 proposes restricting interconnect to a handful of preauthorized chips and says the mechanism 'does not yet exist'; flexHEG proposes verifying cluster configuration and outgoing bandwidth). Training slowdown when traffic is forced off NVLink: no work (RAND's 'might not present a significant performance penalty' is speculation).
