P124 Validity of compute as the governed quantity

Does frontier capability still require large-scale training, and how fast does algorithmic progress make a fixed compute threshold obsolete?

Whether compute remains a valid proxy for capability.

Terms: N: model size. M: total communication between pods.

Status

Posed

Posed by

  • Aarne & Petrie (2025), International Security Applications of Flexible Hardware-Enabled Guarantees (flexHEG Part 3): algorithmic progress could eventually mean that even computations that fall below this initial FLOP threshold could be dangerous. (App A, Ratchets, p52, L2373–2374)

Also raised in: Baker, Kulp, Marks, Brundage & Heim (2025), Verifying International Agreements on AI: Six Layers of Verification for Rules on Large-Scale AI Development and Deployment (RAND WR-A4077-1); Scher & Thiergart (2024), Mechanisms to Verify International Agreements About AI Development (MIRI); Shavit (2023), What Does It Take to Catch a Chinchilla? Verifying Rules on Large-Scale Neural Network Training via Compute Monitoring; Scher (2026), Verifying Restrictions on Frontier AI Research (MIRI); Proofworks, Living Verification Documents.
