P025 Digital perimeters, egress accounting and inference-site architecture

Can a digital-perimeter and egress-accounting design show, to stated confidence, that every outbound message from an inference site has a log entry?

Digital perimeters, egress accounting and inference-site architecture.

Status

Started Hashing every frame on one 400GbE link with generator traffic on real hardware: line rate holds for frames of 1500 B and above on a 32-core CPU, fails for 64 B frames and on DPU offload - a prover choosing small frames defeats complete hashing. This is the capture layer only; reconciling hashed egress against an inference log to a stated confidence, red-team exfiltration tests, and the inference-site architecture (weights path over partitioned networks and data diodes, where 'we have not seen any work') are paper only or no work. The designs are Cankaya's north-south taps with all egress 'fully explainable' by observed activity and Rinberg's Merkle inference ledger. The same benchmark is the evidence for the traffic-hashing problem.

For worst-case 64B minimum frames, no algorithm on this hardware reaches full line rate … For realistic traffic profiles at 1500B and above, AES-128-GMAC and SipHash-1-3-128 are both capable of sustaining line-rate hashing
Reynolds & Foster (2026), Network Traffic Hashing (Amodo Design note) (2026-07-03), L545–547
