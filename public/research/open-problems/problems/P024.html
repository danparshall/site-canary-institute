P024 Data in and out of sealed verification environments

Can data be moved into a sealed verification facility, and results out, while bounding what leaks, including through faults and retries?

Protocols for moving data into, deleting it from, and bounding leakage out of a sealed auditing facility (including one-bit outputs and fault budgets).

Terms: SCIF (sensitive compartmented information facility): a shielded, access-controlled room built to stop information leaking out. air-gap: physically isolating a system from outside networks.

Status

Analyzed Design only: a neutrally located, mutually monitored and possibly air-gapped data centre runs privacy-preserving evaluations, assessors emit one bit per judgment, and the sensitive data is verifiably deleted afterwards (Fig. 2.1 caption); how the bit is transferred is left to future work (fn 261). The fault-and-retry leakage budget appears only as a stated requirement in Cankaya's self-deprecated System Overview v0.2 (a pre-agreed budgeted fault rate). Nothing built, no red-team, no measured leak bound.

After conducting their evaluations as agreed by the Prover and Verifier, the assessor would be able to reliably transmit one bit per judgment to the Verifier and Prover.
Harack et al. (2025), Verification for International AI Governance (Oxford AIGI) (2025-07-03), L4437–4439
