P045 Detection thresholds for undeclared facilities

What is the smallest undeclared facility that combined national technical means and supply-chain tracking can detect with high probability and no prior knowledge of its location?

Smallest undeclared facility that can be detected.

Status

Analyzed A literature-based estimate assuming the facility is not deliberately hidden; the same source says that with active concealment facilities of 10,000 to 100,000 H100-equivalents cannot be ruled out, that the 10,000 figure rests on a single open analysis and 'does not establish a lower bound on the smallest detectable facility', and that the real bound depends on what intelligence agencies achieve. No detection probability is stated, so this survey's detection goal of 90% is not shown. Supply-chain clause: a desk bound of 'on the order of a million chips unaccounted for' from Epoch's chip tracing. Nothing built or tested; the underlying classifier (Clymer 2025) is not held.

"In conclusion, facilities at 10,000 H100-equivalents are still mostly detectable, primarily through satellite surveillance. Combining methods that are independent of each other raises the detection probability further. Below this scale, distinguishability drops sharply and detection becomes much harder."
Koopmanschap & Barten (2026), How to Catch a GPU: A Taxonomy of Verification and Enforcement Mechanisms for International AI Agreements (arXiv 2607.22619v1), §5.3.1 (2026 (arXiv 2607.22619v1; stamp reads 16 Jun 2026)), L478–481
