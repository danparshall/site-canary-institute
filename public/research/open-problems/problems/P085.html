P085 Inference verification for closed-weight models in production

Can inference be verified for closed-weight models on a lab's production stack?

Verifying inference for closed-weight models on a lab's own production serving stack, e.g. inside an attested enclave.

Status

Analyzed Design only: the AI 2040 and Amodo plan verifies production inference by tapping the prover's network and recomputing on a verifier-trusted server placed inside the prover's data centre, so closed weights never leave; the SITREP marks recomputation-server security 'Not on track'. Amodo asks labs to re-implement its open-weight demo on their own stacks with frontier models (not done); Karvonen et al. state DiFR cannot verify closed-weight providers (a design limit, not a test); Cankaya says proprietary-model verification would need third-party- or hardware-attested deployment (one sentence).

Whilst simpler than developing a whole SL5 cluster, the recomputation server needs to sit within the prover's data centre, possibly under their physical control.
Amodo Design (2026), AI 2040 Plan A: Verification SITREP (2026-07-02), L131
