P074 Guarantee-checking logic and its limits

What are the limits of automated guarantee-checking of workloads, and what recomputation fraction is needed to catch a falsified kernel?

Limits of automated guarantee checking of workloads.

Status

Analyzed Proposals and analysis only: random partial recomputation by the guarantee processor, local LLM-based checks, and the authors' own argument that fully automatic on-device compliance checking 'appears unlikely to be feasible'; Reuter, Marks & Baker (2026) give a formal framing (a policy over an algebraic graph; 'functional exclusion' left open). No recomputation fraction has been derived or measured for any detection probability; no automated checker has been run on camouflaged logs. Stated limits: static graphs cannot express mixture-of-experts routing; the relevance of Rice's theorem is 'unclear'.

a general-purpose way to verify each atomic computation is to use the guarantee processor to randomly re-compute a portion of each result
Petrie & Aarne (2025), Technical Options for Flexible Hardware-Enabled Guarantees (flexHEG Part 2) (2025-04 (April 2025 report; arXiv v1 2025-06-03)), L1421–1423
