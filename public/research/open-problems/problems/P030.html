P030 Tap placement and coverage

Where must taps sit to cover the communication paths that matter, and what fraction of compute can run undeclared work invisible to taps at a given network level?

Where taps must sit and what coverage they give.

Terms: q: the probability that an evader's traffic escapes the taps.

Status

Analyzed Desk analysis of tap placement (north-south edge, storage fabric, east-west levels) and what each level misses; names copper scale-up racks (NVL72, NVL576) and TPU pods above 9,000 chips as blind spots where a frontier run could complete without crossing a tap; the covert-bandwidth figures are Fermi estimates the author calls informed guesses; no fraction-of-compute figure and no measurement; the author lists a rigorous threat model of covert workloads under each placement as his most important open question.

For most deployments, we expect spine-to-leaf to be the lowest practical level for tapping intra-SU/pod compute fabric.
Cankaya (2026), Research Note: The Fundamentals and Feasibility of Secure Network Taps for Verifying AI Datacenter Use (Substack), section 3.5.2 (2026-04-30 (live page read 2026-10-21)), L165
