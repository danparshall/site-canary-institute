P055 Keys and hardware roots of trust

What lab cost and time are needed to extract an accelerator's device/attestation key, and can keys be provisioned so neither party holds a copy?

Extraction resistance and provisioning of device keys and roots of trust.

Terms: root of trust (RoT): a small, highly trusted hardware component that the rest of a system's security is built on. HSM (hardware security module): a tamper-resistant device that stores and uses cryptographic keys. Caliptra, OpenTitan: open hardware root-of-trust designs.
