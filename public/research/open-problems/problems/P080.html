P080 Bit-exact training replay

What fraction of training steps replay bitwise on a verifier's hardware, and what throughput penalty does deterministic training impose?

Bit-exact replay of training steps.

Terms: NCCL: Nvidia's library for exchanging data between GPUs during training. ZeRO-3: a technique that splits a model's parameters across many GPUs to save memory.

Status

Started Built and measured: single-GPU training of small models (ResNet-50, 23M parameters, from scratch; GPT-2, 117M, fine-tune) replayed bit-identically across three NVIDIA consumer and workstation GPUs (A40, Titan XP, RTX 2080 Ti), FP64 with logged rounding decisions, at about 1.2-1.4x trainer and 1.3-1.7x auditor time and 18-20 MB of logs per GPT-2 step. Not multi-GPU or distributed (all-reduce ordering unhandled), not low precision, not frontier scale. Throughput-penalty clause: one measured figure elsewhere, Peigné-Lefebvre et al. 2026 report full determinism on a 7B model on 8 H100s costing 1.6-8.2% per step and cite 23-82% overhead for deterministic FlashAttention backward. Fraction-of-steps clause at frontier scale: no work.

A method for two parties, training the same model on different GPU types, to achieve identical training results by logging and sharing rounding decisions
