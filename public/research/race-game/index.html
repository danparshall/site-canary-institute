When should we pause? The AI stampede, with and without verification

Two states can each Build a superintelligence at full speed or Pause. Each thinks a full-speed build has a chance H of causing a catastrophe, and rates that catastrophe at Vc times the value of winning. Notes

Reading the panel. Payoffs are in wins (a safe win = 1, mutual Pause = 0). Outlined cells are the equilibria. The last line is the confidence a state needs that its rival is pausing before pausing is its own best move: 100% on the Stag line, 50% on the risk-dominance line, 0% at the Harmony line.

Model. Two symmetric states choose Build or Pause, after Papenhausen's The AI Race is Not a Prisoner's Dilemma (LessWrong, 2026), with the loser's-project hazard η from his appendix and the logistic race of Roussel et al., Are we Doomed to an AI Race? Why Self-Interest Could Drive Countries Towards a Moratorium on Superintelligence (arXiv:2605.01297, 2026). Under verification a cheat is caught with probability ε, after which the other side takes the better of staying paused and racing from its head start behind; a known cheater pays the punishment, in wins. Race noise is Roussel's σ: a lead L wins with probability 1/(1 + e−L/σ); The game names are the standard game-theory ones (Prisoner's Dilemma, Stag Hunt, Harmony, Deadlock, Chicken), by which pairs of moves are equilibria; risk dominance follows Harsanyi & Selten.
